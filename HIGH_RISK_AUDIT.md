# High-Risk Audit — First Review Set

This is the first audit queue for the manuscript. These entries should be reviewed before lower-risk myths because an inaccurate explanation could delay treatment, alter medication use, affect vaccination decisions, or cause harm during pregnancy or an emergency.

## Priority 1: urgent treatment and emergency response

Review first: `GEN-031`, `GEN-039`, `GEN-058`, `GEN-059`, `KID-087`, `KID-088`, `KID-089`, `KID-093`, `IMP-005`, `IMP-009`, `IMP-022`, `IMP-023`, `IMP-034`, `IMP-035`, `IMP-064`, `IMP-083`.

For each, verify the first-aid sequence, emergency thresholds, contraindications, and local emergency guidance. Confirm that the wording cannot be read as permission to delay emergency care.

## Priority 2: cancer diagnosis and treatment

Review first: `GEN-011`–`GEN-029`, `KID-066`–`KID-075`, `KID-102`, `CAN-001`–`CAN-018`.

Check screening recommendations, diagnostic terminology, risk-factor language, biopsy and surgery claims, treatment claims, and statements about environmental exposures. Confirm that no entry gives false reassurance or presents an unproven therapy as an alternative to oncology care.

## Priority 3: pregnancy, contraception, infants, and children

Review first: `REP-004`–`REP-027`, `KID-083`–`KID-084`, `KID-101`, `IMP-008`, `IMP-014`–`IMP-015`, `IMP-020`, `IMP-024`.

Check gestational age, breastfeeding and contraception limits, medication safety, fever thresholds, vaccine advice, infant feeding language, and age-specific dosing. Verify that “safe” and “unsafe” are not stated as universal when risk depends on dose, trimester, age, or medical history.

## Priority 4: vaccines, medicines, and supplements

Review first: `GEN-049`, `GEN-068`–`GEN-075`, `IMP-001`–`IMP-003`, `IMP-011`, `IMP-040`, `IMP-043`, `IMP-049`–`IMP-050`, `IMP-066`–`IMP-068`, `IMP-075`, `IMP-087`, `IMP-091`, `IMP-093`, `KID-042`, `KID-051`.

Check current recommendations, interaction and adverse-effect warnings, withdrawal cautions, product-quality claims, and whether the entry distinguishes prevention from treatment. Confirm that vaccine and medication statements use current national or specialty guidance.

## Priority 5: nutrition and weight claims

Review first: `GEN-036`, `REP-009`, `REP-010`, `REP-015`, `REP-023`, `REP-049`, `KID-020`, `KID-031`, `KID-056`, `KID-100`, `CAN-018`, `IMP-045`–`IMP-046`, `IMP-072`, `IMP-089`, `IMP-088`.

Check whether the evidence is observational or causal, whether portion and dietary context matter, and whether the wording could promote restrictive eating or unsafe weight loss. Replace “healthy” or “toxic” generalizations with measurable, qualified statements.

## Audit recording format

For each reviewed entry, record: ID, reviewer, date, status decision, citation checked, factual correction needed, safety correction needed, copy-edit correction needed, and approval state (`Pending`, `Needs revision`, or `Approved`).

This queue identifies what to review first; it is not a substitute for physician or pharmacist review.
